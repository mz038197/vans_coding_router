# Portal pool rows match the Ollama settings monthly meter

`GET https://ollama.com/api/usage` now returns daily `usage_usd` buckets, not `limits.monthly.usage`. Each `ollama_cloud` key row shows the settings-page sentence: `$X of $Y used` and `Resets in …`. Spend is the sum of daily `usage_usd` from the subscription anniversary through today, over `range=30d`. The dollar cap is the published included amount for `POST /api/me` plan `pro` ($60), `max` ($300), or `team` ($1000). The anniversary is `SubscriptionRenewsAt` or `renews_at` on `POST /api/me`. Account `CreatedAt` is not that anniversary: this Pro account was created on the 19th, while the settings meter resets on the 21st, and summing from the 19th showed `$7.87 of $60 used` against the settings page's `$2.14 of $60 used`. Without the anniversary the row stays unavailable. A 30-day window that starts after that anniversary is unavailable rather than a short count. This supersedes the fraction display in ADR 0017.
